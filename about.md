Hi — I'm **Imad**, and this is my astrophotography journal.

I use this little corner of the internet to log the night sky one frame at a
time: what I pointed at, how I shot it, and what I learned. It's inspired by the
wonderfully plain style of [APOD](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/) — the picture
comes first, the words come second.
